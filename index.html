Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our phones, exchanges, or repair warranties? We’ve got answers.

Are all smartphones sold at Ambika Mobile 100% genuine with warranty?
Yes! Every smartphone we sell from Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, and Vivo is 100% genuine, factory-sealed, and comes with full official brand manufacturer warranty and support.
Do you offer phone exchange and trade-in deals?
Absolutely. Bring your old smartphone to either our Hazira or Thatipur branch for an instant, transparent evaluation and get the best exchange value towards your new upgrade.
How long does a screen or battery repair take?
Most standard screen and battery replacements are completed in 30 to 60 minutes. You can relax at our store lounge while our expert technicians service your device.
Do repair services come with a warranty?
Yes, all screen and battery replacements include a 6-month service warranty covering parts and craftsmanship.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), cash, and offer zero-cost EMI options through leading finance partners.
